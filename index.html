Hermes Workspace Bridge

This is a personal-use OAuth client used by a private AI assistant to access the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Contacts). It is not distributed, not listed in any store, and used by a single person.

The assistant runs on a private server owned by the same person. Requests go directly to Google's APIs with the owner's consent; no data is sold, processed by third parties, or replicated anywhere else.
